## If you only have 30 seconds
I'm not just "easily angered". My pattern: **fast thinking → high expectations → reality doesn't match → frustration → pushing harder → exhaustion → ability drops → even more frustration.**
The key isn't "stop being angry", it's managing the chain: **expectation → current capacity → frustration → effort → exhaustion → emotion regulation.**

## What I want (core)
1. Succeed at my standard, without burning energy fighting reality.
2. Be able to pause without feeling like giving up / failing.
3. Have automatic ways (muscle memory) so I don't overthink every time.
4. Be accompanied / understood at my pace, or at least not feel lonely when it doesn't happen.
5. Professional validation: is this pattern consistent?
