# 06 — Strict Internal Rule [EN]

Part of: [[03_Pola_Diri_Utama_EN|03 Core Pattern]] • Indonesian: [[06_Aturan_Internal_Keras|06 ID]]

## Core
My unwritten rule: **"If something can actually be done, it should be done."**

Applies to myself AND to others.

## Why it's heavy
Human ability isn't constant. I / others can understand yet still err when:
tired, stressed, losing focus, lacking experience, or using the wrong method.

My rule ignores those conditions.

## Effects
- To self: harsh, forcing, "I should already be able to do this".
- To others: uncomfortable with slow people, eventually leaving → lonely.
