# 07 — Reframe: Not Yet Doesn't Mean Failure [EN]

Part of: [[03_Pola_Diri_Utama_EN|03 Core Pattern]] • Indonesian: [[07_Reframe_Belum_Bisa_Bukan_Gagal|07 ID]]

## Replace these sentences

Old → New:
- "I should already be able to do this." → **"I can't yet in my current condition. What is blocking me?"**
- "I'm incapable." → **"I can't yet."**
- "I failed." → **"It's not finished yet."**
- "It should be done by now." → **"My timeline vs reality differs. What's the technical cause + what's the emotional pressure?"**

## Why it matters
"Should" adds a second pressure: besides the technical problem, you also fight the reality that it's unfinished. Energy drains there instead of into solutions.

Goal: spend energy solving the problem, not fighting the fact it's unsolved.
