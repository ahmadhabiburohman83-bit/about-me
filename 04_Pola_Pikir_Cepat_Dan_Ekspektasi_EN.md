# 04 — Fast Thinking and Expectations [EN]

Part of: [[03_Pola_Diri_Utama_EN|03 Core Pattern]] • Indonesian: [[04_Pola_Pikir_Cepat_Dan_Ekspektasi|04 ID]]

## Core
When I understand something as "simple", my brain instantly sets an expectation: this should be done fast.

## Explanation
- Toward others: "Why don't you get it? It's just like this, this, and this."
- Toward myself: "I'm so stupid." when I err on something easy.

Same pattern, different target.

## Examples from my notes
- Annoyed when others are slow to grasp what I find simple.
- Angry at myself for easy mistakes, then immediately hunting the cause and trying another way.

## Watch out
Expectations are built from **speed of understanding**, not from **actual conditions** (tiredness, stress, focus, experience, method).
