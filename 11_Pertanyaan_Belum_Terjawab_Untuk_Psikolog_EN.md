## Priority for my psychologist (3)

1. **No. 8 — self-acceptance.** If I have tried my absolute hardest but still can't reach what I want, can I still accept myself in that moment? This separates whether the core is perfectionism, need for control, high self-standards, frustration tolerance, or something else.
2. **Group 3 — "so stupid".** When I say that, what am I actually angry about (failing / wasting time / ability not matching expectation)? After the anger: more motivated or more pressured?
3. **Group 7 — limits.** How do I tell "can't yet" apart from "too tired to be able"? What are the early signs before frustration turns into big anger? Why do I keep forcing myself when already tired?

## 1. About "pace"

- What exactly makes me feel my pace differs from others?
- Do I actually process information faster, or am I just faster at deciding what to do?
- In which situations is the pace gap felt most?

## 2. About expectations

- Where does the belief **"I should already be able to do this"** come from?
- Who first taught or shaped such a standard in me?
- Do I feel I must always be capable once I know how?
- What do I feel when reality doesn't match the timeline in my head?

## 3. About anger at myself

- When I say **"so stupid"**, what am I actually angry about?
- Am I angry about failing, wasting time, or ability not matching expectation?
- After anger at myself: more motivated or increasingly pressured?

## 4. About others

- What annoys me most when someone is slow: they don't understand, they don't try, or I have to wait?
- If they are slow but willing to listen and keep trying, how bothered am I still?
- Do I need someone **as fast as me**, or someone **who can understand how I think**?

## 5. About loneliness

- When I leave someone I deem too slow, what do I actually feel afterwards?
- Do I feel relieved, lonely, or both?
- Do I want someone who can match my pace, or someone who makes me feel understood?

## 6. About "venting"

- What did my psychologist actually mean by me having no outlet?
- When pressure builds up, where do I usually release it?
- Which activity truly lowers my pressure, not just distracts?

## 7. About limits

- How do I know I'm **"not able yet"** vs **"too tired to be able"**?
- What are the early signs before frustration turns into big anger?
- Why do I tend to keep forcing myself even when tired?

## 8. The most important question

> **If I have tried my absolute hardest but still can't reach something I want, can I still accept myself in that moment?**
