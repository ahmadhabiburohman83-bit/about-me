# 09 — Pattern With Others and Loneliness [EN]

Part of: [[03_Pola_Diri_Utama_EN|03 Core Pattern]] • Indonesian: [[09_Pola_Ke_Orang_Lain_Dan_Rasa_Sepi|09 ID]]

## Core
I'm uncomfortable when others' pace is much slower. If the gap is too big: annoyed → leave → lonely because few match my pace.

## Hypothesis from my notes
Maybe the problem isn't "others are too slow", but my strict internal standard applied to them too.

I can stay patient if they truly try — I'll find another way. My patience breaks when things **never reach the standard I think should be reached.**

## Questions for my psychologist
- Is "leaving" a regulation strategy (avoiding frustration) or a genuine pace need?
- What does the loneliness cost me?
