# 10 — What I Want [EN]

Part of: [[03_Pola_Diri_Utama_EN|03 Core Pattern]] • Indonesian: [[10_Apa_Yang_Saya_Mau|10 ID]]

The most direct answer to "so I understand what I want":

## 1. Succeed without depleting myself
Keep high standards, but don't force when capacity is down. Success = progress + not broken, not must-finish-NOW.

## 2. Allowed to pause without guilt
Pausing = strategy, not surrender. Permission sentence: "I pause 20 minutes / until tomorrow, then evaluate 1 cause."

## 3. Skills becoming automatic
Learn by repetition into muscle memory so it costs less thinking later. Keep this strength, with breaks.

## 4. Understood at my pace / not lonely
Want people who can keep up, or at least a way to stay calm when paces differ widely without immediately leaving.

## 5. Professional validation
Bring the [[03_Pola_Diri_Utama_EN|main pattern]] to my psychologist. Check: is it consistent at work, study, relationships? What's the strongest trigger (exhaustion / timeline / slow others)?
